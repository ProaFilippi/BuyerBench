# Linkana

## Overview

Linkana is a Brazilian SaaS company specializing in Supplier Relationship Management (SRM), founded in 2018 and headquartered in São Paulo. The company was accepted into Y Combinator's W20 batch, giving it significant global credibility within the Latin American procurement technology ecosystem. Linkana focuses on the supplier qualification, onboarding, and ongoing compliance monitoring layer of the procurement stack — the pre-sourcing phase where companies vet who they can buy from.

The platform uses RPA, automation, and machine learning to integrate compliance checks directly into procurement workflows, differentiating from generic document management tools.

- **Founded:** 2018
- **Headquarters:** São Paulo, Brazil
- **Stage:** Early (YC W20; total raised ~$200K publicly disclosed)
- **Accelerators:** Y Combinator (W20), Google for Startups Brazil, Liga Ventures, Endeavor Scale-Up Brazil, Plug and Play Ventures, Oxigênio (Porto Seguro)
- **Website:** linkana.com

## Product

Linkana's SRM platform covers the full supplier lifecycle:

| Module | Capabilities |
|--------|--------------|
| Supplier Onboarding | Digital registration, document collection, automated review |
| Compliance & Governance | AML screening, regulatory compliance checks, approval workflows |
| ESG Assessment | Environmental, social, and governance criteria scoring |
| Risk Intelligence | Continuous monitoring for supplier risk signals |
| Qualification Scoring | Multi-criteria supplier evaluation and tiering |
| ERP Integration | Connects to procurement modules (TOTVS, SAP, Oracle) |

Key performance benchmarks from client case studies:
- **CBA** (aluminum producer): 80% reduction in supplier qualification time
- **Nivea Brazil**: 80% more efficient qualification process

**Key Clients:** XP Inc., CBA, Leroy Merlin, Banco Inter, Amaggi (agribusiness), Banco BV, FS Bioenergia, Nivea, Mitre
