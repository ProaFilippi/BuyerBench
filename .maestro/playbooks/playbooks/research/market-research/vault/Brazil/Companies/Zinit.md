# Zinit

## Overview

Zinit is an AI/ML-powered e-sourcing SaaS platform focused on corporate procurement automation. Founded in 2023 by Anton Buzdalin and Andrey Chernogorov (both with backgrounds in procurement technology), the company is headquartered in Dubai with active operations in India, Indonesia, Malaysia, and the Middle East — and entered Brazil as a strategic expansion market in 2025. Brazil is now its primary growth focus.

Zinit's core thesis: "tail spend" (recurring low-value purchases) is massively underserved by traditional ERP procurement modules, creating a greenfield opportunity for an ML-native platform. It connects buyers to a network of 25+ million suppliers and handles the full sourcing cycle — RFQ creation, supplier matching, proposal evaluation, negotiation, and contract execution — within a single system.

- **Founded:** 2023
- **Headquarters:** Dubai, UAE (Brazil operations: São Paulo)
- **Stage:** Seed ($8M / ~R$ 44M)
- **Valuation:** US$ 48 million post-seed
- **Website:** zinit.com (operational)

## Product

| Capability | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| Supplier Network | 25+ million suppliers across global database |
| AI Matching | ML-powered supplier identification based on spend category and geography |
| RFQ Automation | Automated proposal solicitation with smart templates |
| Negotiation Engine | AI-assisted negotiation with pricing algorithms |
| Proposal Scoring | Automated multi-criteria evaluation of supplier responses |
| Contract Generation | Automated contracting from approved supplier selection |

Zinit targets **indirect procurement** — non-strategic purchases (maintenance, office supplies, professional services, IT accessories) rather than direct materials sourcing. This is a deliberate market segment choice, avoiding head-on competition with major ERP modules like TOTVS Suprimentos or SAP Ariba.

Claimed outcomes: 20–30% cost savings in indirect spend, significant reduction in procurement cycle time.
