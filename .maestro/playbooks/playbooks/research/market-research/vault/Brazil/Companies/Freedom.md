# Freedom

## Overview

Freedom is a Brazilian AI agents startup founded in late 2024, headquartered in São Paulo. The company experienced exceptional growth — 900% annual revenue increase in 2025 and 3,650% monthly revenue growth — making it one of the fastest-growing AI startups in Brazil. Freedom positions itself as selling "digital labor": deploying AI agents that automate manual processes and centralize fragmented organizational data.

The company acquired Nalk (a marketing and sales data management platform) in early 2026, nearly doubling its client base in a single transaction.

- **Founded:** ~Q4 2024 (shaped Sep 2024 – Jan 2025)
- **Headquarters:** São Paulo, Brazil
- **Stage:** Seed (R$ 14.5 million raised)
- **Website:** Not disclosed in sources

## Product

Freedom builds custom AI agent suites for medium and large enterprises. Key functional areas:

| Area | Agent Capabilities |
|------|--------------------|
| Finance | Invoice automation, expense management, AP/AR workflows |
| Sales | Lead qualification, outreach automation, CRM data hygiene |
| HR | Onboarding workflows, document processing, policy Q&A |
| Operations | Procurement request routing, supplier coordination, process orchestration |

The platform emphasizes **horizontal coverage** — agents that work across all business functions rather than a single vertical — making it comparable globally to platforms like Glean, Moveworks, or Harvey AI but targeting the Brazilian market specifically.

## Target Market

- **Primary:** Medium to large Brazilian enterprises (500–5,000 employees)
- **Key Clients:** Panvel (pharmacy chain), Vibra (energy), Grupo Cartão de Todos (healthcare benefits), Comerc (energy commercialization)
- **Revenue Target:** R$ 500 million within 5 years
