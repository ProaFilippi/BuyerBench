# Pipefy

## Overview

Pipefy is one of Brazil's most successful enterprise software exports — a no-code workflow automation platform founded in 2015 in Curitiba, Paraná, now headquartered across Curitiba and San Francisco. With ~$150 million raised and ~4,000 enterprise clients in 150+ countries, Pipefy is among the most globally scaled Brazilian B2B SaaS companies alongside TOTVS and Stefanini.

In March 2025, Pipefy launched its **AI Agents** product layer (AI Agents 2.0), adding autonomous AI workers alongside its no-code workflow builder. The company deploys 2,600+ AI agents monthly as of early 2025.

- **Founded:** 2015, Curitiba, Brazil
- **Headquarters:** Curitiba + San Francisco
- **Stage:** Late Series B / pre-IPO (~$150M raised)
- **Website:** pipefy.com
- **Team Size:** 400+

## Product

Pipefy's platform spans workflow orchestration and AI automation:

| Layer | Capabilities |
|-------|--------------|
| No-Code Workflows | Visual workflow builder, approval chains, SLA management |
| AI Agents 2.0 | Autonomous task agents; contract validation, document review, routing |
| AI Doc Reader | Extracts structured data from invoices, contracts, PDFs |
| AI Assistants | Conversational policy Q&A ("What is the procurement policy?") |
| CRM AI Studio | Sales process automation with AI |
| Procurement Module | Centralized PR/PO management, approval workflows, supplier request routing |

**Procurement-specific capabilities:**
- Procurement Policy Assistant: answers internal purchasing questions, can auto-initiate requests
- Contract validation agents
- Request intake and routing
- Spend approval workflows with configurable rules

**AI infrastructure:** The platform is model-agnostic — agents can run on OpenAI, Meta Llama, or Google Gemini models, positioned as an "agent orchestrator."

**Scale metrics (2025):**
- 1,600 agents operating weekly across client base
- 2,600 agents deployed in February 2025 alone
- 85% of workflows built by non-technical users
